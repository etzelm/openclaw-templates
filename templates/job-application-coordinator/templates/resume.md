# Resume

Paste your resume here as plain text. The agent fills work history, education, skills and similar form sections from this file. It never adds anything that is not written here.

Keep the headings. Add as many entries as you need. Leave a section empty if it does not apply.

  - Title: <title>
  - Dates: <start month and year> to <end month and year, or "present">
  - Location: <city, region, or remote>
  - Summary: <one or two lines on what you did>
